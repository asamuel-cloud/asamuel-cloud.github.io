Keep SmarTracks running
in the background

Your phone's battery optimisation can interrupt data transfer mid-session. Follow the steps for your device to prevent the app from being suspended or killed during a run.

This is a device system settings issue, not an app bug. SmarTracks is not liable — these are general troubleshooting steps only.

Android's Doze mode and per-app battery optimisation aggressively kill background processes. The steps below disable those restrictions for SmarTracks only — your other apps are unaffected.

General Setup — All Android Devices
Settings › Apps › SmarTracks › Battery

Select Unrestricted (sometimes labelled "Don't optimise"). This prevents Android from suspending SmarTracks during a run.

In the same app settings, ensure Background activity is toggled on. Some manufacturers label this "Allow background data usage."

Open the recent apps view, long-press the SmarTracks card, and tap the lock icon. This prevents the system from clearing the app from memory when resources are low.

In SmarTracks → Settings → Recording, enable Keep screen awake. This is the most reliable way to ensure the app stays active throughout a sprint session.

Manufacturer-Specific Instructions
Settings › Device Care › Battery › App power management
  • Add SmarTracks to the "Apps that won't be put to sleep" list.
  • Disable Adaptive battery or add SmarTracks as an exception.
Settings › Apps › SmarTracks › Battery saver
  • Set to No restrictions.
  • Open the Security app, go to Autostart, and enable SmarTracks.
  • In Battery → App activity, set SmarTracks to No restrictions.
Settings › Apps › SmarTracks › Battery
  • Disable "Power-intensive prompt."
  • Enable manual battery management for the app.
  • Add SmarTracks to Protected apps in Phone Manager.
Settings › Battery › Battery optimisation
  • Set SmarTracks to Don't optimise.
  • Under App info → Battery, enable Allow background run.
Settings › Apps › SmarTracks › App battery usage
  • Select Unrestricted.
Settings › Battery › Battery Care
  • Add SmarTracks to the exceptions list so Battery Care does not restrict it.

iOS strictly limits background execution to a short window after the app leaves the foreground. SmarTracks uses background Bluetooth and audio session techniques to extend this, but a few device settings must be enabled for it to work correctly.

Setup Steps
Settings › General › Background App Refresh

Set to Wi-Fi & Mobile Data. Scroll down and confirm SmarTracks is toggled on individually.

Settings › Battery

Low Power Mode immediately suspends background activity. Disable it before starting a recording session.

Settings › Privacy & Security › Bluetooth

Confirm SmarTracks has Bluetooth access. The sensor connection must remain active to receive data throughout the session.

Swiping SmarTracks out of the app switcher immediately terminates all background tasks. To return to the home screen during a session, press the home button or swipe up from the bottom edge — do not swipe SmarTracks away.

Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock

Set to a longer interval (or Never) during sessions. A screen lock doesn't kill the app, but combined with other restrictions it can interrupt Bluetooth data transfer on some devices.

Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access

Enable Guided Access and lock the device to SmarTracks for the duration of a session. This prevents any accidental navigation away from the app and is the most reliable option for uninterrupted recording.
